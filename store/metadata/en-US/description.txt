Find a little time for chess each day. Chess Room brings offline play, short tactics sessions and opening practice together in one app.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
• Challenge AI with adjustable difficulty, side and time controls.
• Play with a friend on the same device.
• Use Quick Play to start an AI match with your last settings.
• Pick up an unfinished match with Continue Game. Your moves and remaining time save automatically.

MAKE PRACTICE A HABIT
• Solve the daily challenge from a rotating collection of 71 positions and build your streak.
• Explore 77 exercises in the themed puzzle library, which includes the daily positions.
• Practice easy, medium and hard challenges covering checkmates, forks, promotions and combinations.
• Play through multi-move solutions, including mate in two. Use hints when you need a nudge, then read a short explanation of the tactic.

LEARN AND REVISIT
• Practice five opening lines and read the in-app guide to the rules.
• Use move hints and optional undo and redo during games.
• Save, replay and review completed games. Import or export games in PGN format.
• Choose board themes and piece styles to make the board your own.

Core chess play and puzzle practice work offline. Your game history and training progress are stored on this device.
